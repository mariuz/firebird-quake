How it works

      
    
        
  • The BSP becomes tables. FACES with their plane and texture mapping, FACE_VERTS
          (the edges and surfedges resolved), LEAVES with a decompressed PVS as a hex string,
          MARKSURFACES, and HULLS: the node tree and both clipnode hulls, one row per node with the
          plane copied in. Alias models contribute MODELS and their animation runs in ANIMS; the
          entity lump becomes MAP_ENTS.
    • 
        
  • Collision is a recursive procedure. RHC is SV_RecursiveHullCheck: it splits the
          segment at every plane it crosses and recurses into both sides, threading the trace state through its parameters.
          TRACE_MOVE runs it against the world and every door and platform, and tests monsters and the player
          with a Minkowski slab test. FLY_MOVE, WALK_MOVE, MOVE_STEP and
          PUSH_MOVE are sv_phys.c in PSQL, clip planes and pushed entities kept in global temporary tables because
          PSQL has no arrays.
    • 
        
  • The game is PSQL. QUAKE_TIC runs at 20 Hz: the player's friction, acceleration, jumping and
          swimming, doors (linked by touching boxes, with keys), platforms, buttons, trains, secret doors, triggers
          (once, multiple, secret, counter, relay, teleport, push, hurt, changelevel), items and weapons from the axe to the
          thunderbolt, damage with armour and quad, gibs, and the monster AI of ai.qc: FIND_TARGET,
          MOVE_TO_GOAL, NEW_CHASE_DIR, CHECK_ATTACK, and the grunt, dog, knight,
          ogre, scrag, fiend, zombie and shambler attacks, plus the registered episodes' enforcer, death knight, vore (homing pods), spawn, rotfish and Shub-Niggurath. Chthon rises in E1M7 and takes the lightning; Shub falls to the telefrag.
    • 
        
  • The renderer is a query. FRAME_FACES finds the leaf the eye is in, marks the leaves in its PVS
          that touch the view frustum, collects their faces (DISTINCT, Quake's visframe), drops back faces and
          faces outside the frustum, and walks each polygon in a single cursor: view transform, clipping to the near plane,
          projection, and the texel coordinates texinfo gives every vertex. Doors, platforms and ammo boxes are brush models
          and go through the same cursor at their own origin. FRAME_ENTS lists the alias models and sprites in
          the PVS with their pose and frame; FRAME_LIGHTSTYLES animates the lights.
    • 
        
  • JavaScript only paints. It rasterises the polygons with perspective-correct spans over a surface cache
          (texture × lightmap through the colormap, as r_surf.c did), draws the alias models with Gouraud light sampled
          from the lightmap below them, sprites, particles, the two-layer sky, and the status bar from gfx.wad.
          Sounds are rows in SOUND_EVENTS, spatialised with the Web Audio API. The map's ambient emitters
          (drips, hums, torches) loop at their entities; the water and wind ambients come from the BSP leaf the player
          is in, which QUAKE_TIC reports each frame, faded as S_UpdateAmbientSounds did.
          Quake's music was CD audio, not in the pak: drop track02.ogg…track11.ogg into
          music/ (or pick that folder) and each map plays its worldspawn track; without them a
          synthesised drone fills in.
    •
